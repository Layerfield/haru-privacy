01Data sent to the developer by the app

Carousel Studio (bundle identifier com.haru.folio) is a photo and text carousel editor. The current app does not collect or transmit your selected photos, text, projects or edit history to a developer server.

It has no registration, advertising, user tracking, or third-party analytics or crash-reporting SDKs. Support email and this website are described separately below.

02What is processed and stored on your device

  • Working copies of photos you select, your text and project titles
  • Photo references, crop position, zoom, filters, frames, layout, and creation and modification times
  • Project documents, last-known-good recovery copies, and generated JPEG and PNG files

Editing and image generation take place on your device. Files remain in the app's local storage. Selected originals may be copied to temporary files during import. The app cleans up those temporary copies after processing. Imported photos are normalized for orientation and converted to resized working JPEGs. Original EXIF and GPS metadata is not copied into those working files. This does not remove personal information visible within a photo or written in your text.

03Choosing and saving photos

The app uses Apple's system photo picker to receive only the images you select. It does not request read access to your entire photo library. If you choose an image stored in iCloud, Apple's system may download it.

The app requests add-only Photos permission when you save finished images to your photo library. You can decline and still edit and export files through the system share sheet. Change permissions in your device's Settings app. The app does not request camera, microphone, location, contacts or tracking permissions.

04Sharing and backups you choose

When you use the system share sheet, exported files can be sent to the app or service you select. That destination's privacy policy and retention settings apply to the shared copy.

There is no app-account cloud sync. Depending on your device settings, local app data or saved photos may be included in iCloud or computer backups, or iCloud Photos. The developer cannot access your private backups. Apple's settings and policies govern those services.

05Retention and deletion

Projects, working photos, recovery copies and successfully generated export files do not automatically expire in app storage. The current app does not have an individual-project deletion or reset-all button.

Use Delete App in device Settings to remove the app's local data from that device. Offload App keeps documents and data. Copies in Photos, Files, sharing destinations and existing backups must be managed separately. See Manage and delete your data for instructions.

06When you email us

The email address, message and attachments you choose to send are used to investigate, reply to and resolve your request. Do not send unnecessary photos, passwords, payment information or other people's personal information. Your mail provider and the recipient's Apple iCloud Mail service may process and store the email.

We retain support information only as needed to handle the request and follow-up, then delete it, subject to any applicable legal retention obligation. Contact salient-buckram.1r@icloud.com to request access, correction or deletion. The developer cannot remotely inspect or delete projects stored only on your device.

07This website

We have not installed advertising, analytics, cookies or input forms on this site. Public pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process IP addresses and access information to deliver and secure the website under the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Opening these pages does not upload your app photos or projects to GitHub. External providers' network and processing policies apply when you open their links or send email.

08Children and policy updates

The app does not request birth dates or children's accounts, or require children to submit personal information to the developer. Contact us if you believe a child's information was sent in a support email.

We update this policy and its date when features or data handling change. Where separate consent is required, the relevant feature will provide the necessary notice and consent. This policy describes the iOS and iPadOS version shown above.
