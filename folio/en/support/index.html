01Make your first story

  1. On the home screen, choose ‘내 사진으로 시작’ to select photos, or try the eight sample photos.
  2. Choose a style and aspect ratio, then tap a photo or text element on a page to edit it.
  3. Open ‘저장 전 확인’ to review layout and text overflow, then generate JPEG or PNG files.
  4. Save to Photos or choose an app or location in the system share sheet.

The app supports iOS and iPadOS 16 or later. System permission screens may vary by device and operating system. Button labels above match the current Korean-language app interface.

02Common questions

I declined Photos permission.

You can still export files and use the system share sheet to save to Files or another destination. To save directly to Photos, allow add-only Photos access for Carousel Studio in device Settings.

A photo will not import.

Check whether the original opens in Photos. An iCloud-only photo may require the system to download it using a network connection. Try another image if the file is damaged or unsupported, and check free storage.

A photo or text looks cut off.

Select that element to adjust photo position or zoom, or shorten the text. Tap a warning in the review screen to go to the affected element.

Does changing the ratio overwrite my project?

Changing the aspect ratio creates a new project copy. You can reopen the original from the home screen's project list.

Can I open a project on another device?

The current app has no account sync or project-package sharing. You can share JPEG and PNG results, but these do not transfer the editable source project.

How do I delete or recover a project?

There is currently no individual-project deletion button. See Manage and delete your data for local app data, saved copies and backups. The developer cannot remotely recover projects from your device.
