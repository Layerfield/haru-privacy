CAROUSEL STUDIO / DATA

Manage and delete your data

There is no app account. Manage projects stored inside the app separately from copies saved or shared elsewhere.

Effective and last updated: October 16, 2026

Applies to: Carousel Studio · iOS / iPadOS · 0.1.1 (2)

On this page

01There is no app account to delete

Carousel Studio has no registration or server account. The developer does not keep your photos or projects on a server, so no server-account deletion request is needed. The instructions below apply to the current iPhone and iPad app.

02Delete data stored inside the app

  1. First export any JPEG or PNG results you want to keep. They are not backups that can restore the complete editable project.
  2. Open Settings → General → iPhone Storage (or iPad Storage) and choose Carousel Studio.
  3. Choose Delete App. Offload App retains documents and data and does not clear everything.

This removes project documents, working photos, recovery copies and export files inside the app on that device. There is currently no button to delete one project or reset all data within the app.

03Check external copies and backups

  • Photos: separately delete saved results in Photos and check Recently Deleted if needed.
  • Files and sharing destinations: manage exported copies in the folder or service where you saved or sent them.
  • Device, iCloud and computer backups: old backups are not automatically removed. Check Apple's backup management settings. Restoring a backup may restore earlier app data.

Deleting the app does not delete all external copies or irreversibly erase every backup.

04Request deletion of support-email information

For access, correction or deletion of personal information you sent to support, email salient-buckram.1r@icloud.com. Include the email address used and approximate date of the original request. We ask only for information needed to identify and verify the request.

We will explain any applicable legal obligation requiring retention. The developer cannot remotely delete projects stored only on your device, your iCloud backups or copies held by another sharing service.
